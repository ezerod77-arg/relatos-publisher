Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026.

Relatos Publisher accesses social media accounts only after explicit user authorization. The application may process account identifiers, authorization tokens, profile information required for account selection, and media publishing status needed to upload and manage authorized content.

Relatos Publisher does not sell personal data. Credentials and authorization tokens are used only to provide the requested publishing functionality and are not intentionally shared with unrelated third parties.

Users may revoke access through the relevant social platform at any time. Data retained for authentication or publishing can be deleted when access is revoked or no longer required for the service.

For privacy inquiries, contact the application owner through the project repository.
