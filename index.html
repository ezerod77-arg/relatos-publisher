Relatos Publisher

Prepare narrated story videos and upload selected files to authorized creator accounts.

What the application does

Relatos Publisher is a desktop publishing tool for localized narrated videos. Its TikTok integration uses an explicit account authorization and sends a selected video to the creator’s TikTok inbox. The creator reviews, edits and decides whether to share it in TikTok.

This website documents the desktop application. It does not sign you in or upload files from this page.

TikTok upload workflow

  1. Open Relatos Publisher on the desktop and choose to connect a TikTok account.
  2. Review the TikTok authorization screen and authorize the requested account information and video-upload permissions.
  3. Check the displayed account name and select the video to upload.
  4. Confirm the upload. Relatos Publisher transfers the file in parts and checks its processing status.
  5. Open the TikTok inbox to review and complete sharing. Uploading a draft does not automatically publish a post.

Permissions and account control

user.info.basic identifies the connected account. video.upload sends the selected video to that account’s inbox. The demonstrated integration does not use Direct Post or request video.publish.

Account authorization can be revoked through TikTok. Only connect accounts you own or are authorized to manage.

Availability

The TikTok upload flow has been demonstrated in TikTok’s sandbox. Production approval is pending; this site does not claim the application is approved or generally available.
